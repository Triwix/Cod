## Screenshot plan

Use a final Release/TestFlight candidate with the approved name. Show only original
example content, without personal images, credentials or unlicensed third-party
material. QA attachments are not finished marketing assets. Do not fabricate PiP
on simulator screenshots when the simulator only displays a black window.

| Order | Suggested caption | Actual capture required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Keep your reference in view | Real photo/text PiP on a physical device over an owner-created reference or neutral screen; capture actual playback. |
| 2 | Photos, videos and documents | Float list with compact media/file choices. |
| 3 | Text that moves with you | Unified Text editor with readable original sample and Teleprompter on; optionally show words mode in a second asset. |

| 6 | Reuse what matters | Library with named original cards/presets and a favorite, without personal history. |
| 7 | Share with fewer steps | Actual Share Shortcut setup/action, clearly labelled as one-time optional setup, not the app-icon route. |

Apple's current specification page lists required iPhone Dynamic Island medium
and iPad 13-inch screenshot groups. It lists 1206×2622 as an accepted iPhone size
and 2064×2752 for iPad 13-inch, with landscape counterparts. Confirm the final
device groups and accepted sizes in App Store Connect before capture/upload;
do not stretch an iPad mini image to pretend it was captured on a 13-inch iPad.
See [Apple's screenshot specifications](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/screenshot-specifications/).

## Account answers — owner must approve

- App name, developer identity, copyright, category, price, storefronts and support
  contact are not decided here. The support page needs genuine contact information.
- The current source has no analytics, tracking, login, advertising or developer
  upload service. Local media processing/preferences alone are not an off-device
  collection service. Reconcile final App Privacy answers with all final code,
  dependencies and the hosted policy; don't treat a privacy manifest as the answers.
- Complete the current age-rating and export-compliance questionnaires; this
  document makes no legal determination and does not assert an age rating.
- Do not claim full-app accessibility support solely because selected large-text
  simulator checks pass. Gesture crop workflows still need assistive-technology
  evaluation before making corresponding product-page accessibility claims.

## Release blockers that local preparation cannot remove

1. Owner's physical-device PiP, media playback and timer-alarm acceptance.
2. Developer Program membership, distribution signing and final App Group identity.
3. Approved hosted privacy policy and support contact, with a working in-app link.
4. Signed archive validation, TestFlight checks and screenshots of the approved app.
5. Owner-approved metadata/questionnaires, then submission and Apple's decision.
